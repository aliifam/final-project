Digitalization has become a driving force behind fundamental transformation in the hospitality industry, necessitating management systems that are not only efficient but also digitally integrated. This phenomenon is equally relevant to capsule hotels—a rapidly growing accommodation segment known for space and cost efficiency. However, these physical advantages are often not matched by robust digital operational systems. In response to this challenge, this study focuses on the design and development of Tamu.id, a comprehensive web-based management application for capsule hotels. The application is built with a multi-user architecture (franchise superadmin, hotel admin, and guest) to support centralized and integrated operational management. From the management side, the system includes features such as booking synchronization with Online Travel Agencies (OTAs), dynamic time-based pricing (temporal pricing), and a Content Management System (CMS) for property information. To support business growth, a flexible promotional scheme is also implemented through referrals and discount vouchers. From the guest experience perspective, the system prioritizes ease and security at every stage. The process begins with seamless authentication via Google OAuth2 (passwordless login). To prevent race conditions during booking, an optimistic locking mechanism is employed. Real-time and universal payment is facilitated using QRIS, integrated via Midtrans webhooks. The highlight of innovation lies in the digital room key system based on chained QR codes—a code regeneration method that ensures reliable key functionality with IoT devices, even in offline conditions. The entire system development process adopts the Agile methodology with a Kanban approach to ensure flexibility and result accuracy. The final outcome of this research is a ready-to-use capsule hotel management platform that significantly enhances operational efficiency for hotel operators while delivering a modern and seamless stay experience for guests.
